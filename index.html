Before we rely on these numbers

  • Legal unit count. The listing is classed as a triplex, but the rent roll shows four apartments. We will confirm permits and zoning with the Town of Aurora. If Apt D is not a legal unit, set its rent to $0 to see the effect.
  • Leases and rent history for all four units and the garage and rear lot. At $2,400 a month, the garage is about 23% of income and the hardest space to re-let.
  • Separate hydro and gas meters for each unit, since tenant-paid utilities depend on them. During a vacancy, those bills fall to the owner. We will also confirm how the tenants' share of water is charged; if it is collected on top of rent, it should be added to income.
  • Repairs budget. $1,600 a year is about 1.3% of rent. Older multiplexes usually need more, plus a reserve for roofs, furnaces and appliances.
  • Land transfer tax uses the provincial multiplex brackets (2% above $400,000). Your lawyer will confirm the final amount.
